# Business Brief

## Situation
A city road safety programme wants to direct enforcement, engineering and campaign effort to where collisions cause the most harm. The 2020 data covers a year in which traffic changed sharply in spring, so the team also needs to know whether 2020 can serve as a baseline.

## Stakeholders and decisions

| Stakeholder | Decision | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| Road safety programme lead | Where to focus enforcement and campaigns | Collisions and injuries by time, borough and factor |
| Traffic engineering lead | Which corridors need engineering review | Top streets and expressways, with injury counts |
| Pedestrian and cycling safety lead | Where to protect vulnerable users | Injuries and deaths by road user type |
| Policy analyst | Whether traffic patterns changed in 2020 | Monthly and daily comparison, with its limits |

## Business questions

| # | Question | Decision it supports | What would change the decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | When do collisions and injuries happen? | Timing of enforcement and campaigns | A time band or month with an injury rate well above the rest |
| Q2 | How do boroughs compare? | Resource allocation | Injury rates per collision, with the unrecorded group shown separately |
| Q3 | Which contributing factors are most common? | Which behaviours to target | A factor that is both common and linked to injury |
| Q4 | Who is injured and killed? | Protection for vulnerable users | Pedestrian or cyclist shares well above their share of injuries |
| Q5 | Which streets and corridors have the most collisions? | Engineering review | Corridors that appear repeatedly across measures |
| Q6 | Did the pattern change between early and spring 2020? | Whether 2020 is a usable baseline | A large, statistically clear change in daily counts |

## Scope
- **In:** collisions, casualties, factors, road users, locations and timing in 2020.
- **Out:** traffic volumes, speed data, population-based rates, and any claim that a specific cause produced the spring change.

## Constraints
- No population or traffic-volume data, so rates are per collision and per day, not per person or per vehicle.
- August covers 1 to 29 August only.
